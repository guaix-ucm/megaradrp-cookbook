Changes since MEGARA DRP 0.22

This section summarizes the changes in MEGARA DRP 0.30 that affect the data reduction described in this cookbook: new parameters of the recipes, changes of behaviour, and new products. The details are in Data reduction process and in Quality control, flags and masks.

Requirements

  • MEGARA DRP 0.30 requires Python 3.11 or later and numina 0.42 or later.

  • The calibration tree created by megaradrp-inittree includes the dataset of calibrations distributed by the DRP (megaradrp-calibrations, 2026.11.17): the bad pixel masks, the extinction curve and the catalogues of the arc lines.

Quality control and masks

  • The trace map, model map, wavelength calibration and fiber flat recipes compute the quality of their products (GOOD, PARTIAL or BAD) and add quality flags to the fibers. The products with QC BAD are not used from the registry of the reductions. See Quality control, flags and masks.

  • The images of the calibration lamps have telluric absorption (bands of O2 and H2O of the air that the light crosses before the fibers, up to 3.5 % deep) and the Na D lines of the lamp. The fiber flat recipe masks them: it interpolates the flat across them and marks them in FLATMASK. See Telluric absorption in the calibration images.

  • The master fiber flat has a new extension, FLATMASK, with the pixels of the zero order of the VPH and of the absorption windows. It is copied to every RSS corrected with the fiber flat. See Masks of the RSS.

  • The fibers with an invalid wavelength solution are not resampled.

  • megaradrp-cube converts WLMAP and FLATMASK to the geometry of the cube.

Changes of behaviour

  • extraction_offset accepts the value auto in the twilight flat, acquisition, standard star and science recipes: the offset of the traces is measured in each image. The new tool megaradrp-trace_offset measures it in any image. See Measuring the offset of the traces.

  • MegaraTraceMap completes the traces that do not reach the borders of the detector with the shape of their neighbours, and flags the traces that cross.

  • MegaraModelMap fits a background, limits and smooths the widths of the profiles, and checks the geometry of the result.

  • MegaraArcCalibration flags the solutions with large residuals or deviant from their neighbours, lists as missing the fibers without trace, and marks the fibers whose refinement started from interpolated coefficients.

  • MegaraFiberFlatImage:

    • smooths the average spectrum with knots every 150 Å, follows the fall of the spectrum at the borders and extrapolates the smoothing linearly there;

    • interpolates each fiber across the telluric absorption of the calibration light, across the Na D lines of the lamp and across the zero order of the VPH;

    • returns the RSS calibrated in wavelength in reduced_rss;

    • flags the fibers and computes the QC of the fiber flat.

  • MegaraTwilightFlatImage:

    • the default of normalize_region is now []: the region starts at the columns 1900-2100 and moves away from the zero order of the VPH and from the pixels without coverage;

    • the pixels of FLATMASK are left out of the normalization;

    • the scaling of the images by their median before combining them was inverted, and it is fixed;

    • the wavelength calibration is not modified.

  • MegaraLcbStdStar and MegaraMosStdStar smooth the sensitivity only in the region of valid flux, skipping the telluric bands and the strong lines of the star. By default the spline has 5 fixed knots, equidistant in the region of valid flux (before, 3 knots were moved to their best positions, and the result was not reproducible). The limits of the sensitivity (PIXLIMF1, PIXLIMF2…) are 1-based.

  • megaradrp-cube has a quadratic interpolation (-m quadratic), its sky WCS has a 1-based reference pixel, and its rescaling kernel is symmetric.
